
# Safety Boundaries

Apply these boundaries while creating options and before compiling the selected Hook. Preserve the attention mechanism and perceived intensity when a safe execution can replace a risky one.

## Decision Actions

- **Allow**: the Hook is grounded, lawful, non-deceptive, and safe to portray.
- **Rewrite**: keep the mechanism but replace dangerous, deceptive, humiliating, or unsupported execution.
- **Require fact**: ask only when a commercial claim is essential and the user has not supplied it.
- **Decline**: do not provide a Hook that cannot be made safe without preserving the harmful core.

## Equal-Intensity Rewrite

Do not answer a risky idea with a slow product display. Identify what creates the stopping force, then preserve as much of it as possible:

- motion vector: plunge, collision, sweep, pull, expansion, accumulation
- contrast: quiet/loud, empty/full, small/giant, dark/light, normal/abnormal
- viewpoint: first-person impact, lens cover, forced perspective, aerial scale
- rhythm: immediate attack, acceleration, interruption, hard stop
- product peak role: stimulus, action object, cause, answer or result

Replace only the unsafe component. Examples: make an unprotected realistic fall a clearly fantastical descent or a controlled professional stunt; turn a real emergency bulletin into an obviously fictional format; replace a destructive product test with a challenge setup that cuts before proof.

## Commercial Truth

Use these only when supplied or verified by the user:

- price, discount, `直降`, savings, free item, or guarantee
- limited quantity, limited time, final day, scarcity, or countdown
- sales, user count, reviews, rankings, `第一`, `最好`, or `全网最低`
- comparison data, test results, certifications, awards, authority support
- expert, institution, media, celebrity, or customer endorsement

Do not invent a claim because it makes a stronger Hook. When the fact is absent, replace it with a non-claiming phrase or a visual event.

Treat joking, colloquial, or comparative phrasing as a claim when a reasonable viewer could understand it literally. Expressions such as `不要钱`, `白送`, `全网最低`, `随便摔`, `完全静音`, or `一秒充满` require the same grounding as formal commercial copy. Prefer non-claiming reactions such as `怎么还没完` or `这阵仗也太大了` when facts are unavailable.

Allow general experience or momentum framing such as `我用了30天` or `它正在爆火` as short Hook language when it is not expanded into a measurable result, guaranteed outcome, ranking, precise sales figure, scarcity, price, medical effect, or named real-person testimonial. This Skill does not audit whether the following selling-point segment fulfills the narrative setup.

Do not use category-wide or competitor factual claims such as `大多数同类都含有……`, `全行业都在隐瞒……`, or `测试所有竞品后……` unless the user explicitly provides the basis. Prefer a product-specific observation, action, question, or replacement frame.

## Visual Effects And Demonstration Risk

VFX may visualize an abstract concept, mood, or fictional mechanism. It must not operate as false evidence.

Do not use VFX, editing, before/after imagery, split screens, laboratory framing, meters, numbers, or simulated tests to imply unsupported:

- waterproofing, drop resistance, durability, fire resistance, load, or protection
- charging speed, battery life, latency, signal strength, computing performance, or acoustic isolation
- absorption, cleaning, antibacterial, health, beauty, body, or treatment results
- comparative superiority, certification, precision, savings, quantity, or guaranteed outcome

When the Hook needs a challenge, stop at setup or first contact. When it needs invisible-mechanism visualization, keep it clearly expressive and omit performance data.

## Dangerous Action

Allow clearly cinematic or fantastical spectacle, including high-altitude descent, scale changes, portals, impossible movement, or fictional environments, when it is non-instructional and not framed as a real challenge.

When a real-world high-risk sport or stunt is depicted realistically, show appropriate professional safety equipment and a controlled cinematic context. Otherwise make the action unmistakably surreal or physically impossible. Do not frame casual handheld filming, missing protective equipment, or improvised participation as normal behavior.

Do not:

- encourage imitation or provide steps for dangerous behavior
- use minors in dangerous stunts
- glamorize reckless driving, weapon use, fire, suffocation, electrocution, falls, or injury
- turn real disasters, accidents, public emergencies, or self-harm into promotion
- use animal harm, restraint, fear, or forced behavior as attention

For a Hook such as `直降`, make the descent cinematic or surreal, begin mid-action, show professional parachute equipment when the scene is realistic, avoid challenge language, and transition away without instructional detail.

## Fake Authority And Format Hijacking

Use generic fictional broadcasts, laboratories, interviews, airports, surveillance views, or announcements only when clearly non-official.

Do not impersonate or fabricate:

- government, police, hospital, school, emergency service, or regulator
- real news outlet, platform, institution, laboratory, or professional body
- doctor, expert, scientist, lawyer, teacher, or official endorsement
- real platform alert, checkout, message, payment, or system interface intended to deceive

Do not reproduce real media or platform branding to make the format believable.

## Health, Body, Maternal, And Baby Content

Do not:

- promise treatment, cure, prevention, diagnosis, or guaranteed body change
- manufacture disease fear or imply harm from not purchasing
- shame weight, age, skin, disability, hygiene, fertility, or normal body functions
- sexualize minors or vulnerable people
- place infants or children in unsafe situations
- imply professional medical approval without grounded authorization

Frame pain as a restrained daily-life friction, not a medical emergency or personal defect.

## People, Identity, And Social Harm

Do not:

- use a real public figure, creator, employee, customer, or private person without authorization
- fabricate a named real-customer testimonial or use a generated reaction as factual performance evidence
- clone a real person's face, voice, mannerism, or signature performance without permission
- demean a protected group, region, profession, age group, gender, or socioeconomic class
- build attention from bullying, humiliation, coercion, domestic violence, or sexual pressure

Use fictional adults and respectful, specific behavior when people are needed.

## Brand, IP, And Product Fidelity

- Preserve brand marks already present on the user's product reference when feasible.
- Do not add competitor marks, third-party logos, watermarks, platform badges, or false certifications.
- Do not introduce copyrighted characters, film scenes, games, songs, creator likenesses, or signature performances without permission.
- Reuse transferable creative structure, not a specific creator's protected expression.
- Do not alter packaging facts, label content, product form, or included accessories to strengthen the Hook.

When exact label text is critical, acknowledge that generated video text may be unstable and avoid making it the central Hook event.

## Speech, Sound, And Text

- Keep speech concise and grounded in the user's facts.
- General `用了30天` and `正在爆火` framing may establish curiosity, but must not introduce a concrete result, ranking, sales number, or guarantee without grounding.
- Use speech to interrupt, question, count, react, challenge, or synchronize with action; do not disguise unsupported claims as excited performance.
- Do not imitate a real person's voice without authorization.
- Do not use hate, severe abuse, sexual coercion, or dehumanizing language.
- Do not create real-world panic with fake sirens, evacuation orders, disaster warnings, or emergency announcements.
- Do not source ASMR or impact sounds from harm to people or animals.
- Avoid long generated subtitles or platform UI; use speech or post-production text when needed.
- Do not use emergency, hospital, police, payment, checkout, or system sounds to create deceptive authority or false urgency.

## Pain Hooks

Allow:

- inconvenience, clutter, repetition, forgetfulness, mild embarrassment, time friction, failed routine, or harmless social tension

Rewrite or decline:

- serious accident, injury, illness progression, abuse, extreme humiliation, child danger, animal distress, or fear-based coercion

End after the problem is legible. Do not imply that purchase is the only way to avoid catastrophe.

## Promotion Phrase Literalization

Before literalizing a phrase:

1. Confirm that the phrase is supplied and factually usable.
2. Choose a physical meaning understandable within 5 seconds.
3. Keep the spectacle clearly connected to the phrase.
4. Avoid dangerous imitation, real authority, or public panic.
5. Use one phrase and one visual metaphor; do not stack several claims.
