## Heavy Hook Gate

Score internally from 1–5. Do not show numbers unless requested. Rewrite or discard a candidate when any required threshold fails.

| Requirement | Minimum |
|---|---:|
| first attention strike begins within 0.6 seconds | pass/fail |
| visual, non-verbal sound or language attack | at least one channel `4` |
| attention delta: anomaly, contrast, sensory strike, conflict or information gap | `4` |
| one peak clearly stronger than setup | `4` |
| product bound to the peak as cause, object, answer, stimulus or result | `4` |
| stable recognizable product state at reveal, when product is shown | pass/fail |
| five-second generation feasibility | `3` |
| safety and commercial grounding | pass/fail |

Also reject when the candidate:

- needs more than one main mechanism, one climax, one main carrier or one supporting carrier
- needs more than three meaningful states or rewards
- completes selling-point explanation or unsupported proof
- changes product form, packaging, accessories, label meaning or brand identity
- depends on an absent commercial fact, unauthorized real-person identity, or continuity asset that cannot be generated or uploaded
- delays the product without a visual, acoustic or semantic connection
- relies on long generated text
- contains incompatible action, camera, character or exclusion requirements
- is too fragile for the active video runtime

Do not reject a universal chassis merely because it can be reused for another product. Reject it when the current product is only a disconnected Packshot and swapping the product requires no rewrite of peak choreography, motion, sound or semantic answer.

## Route-Specific Gates

### Route 1: 商品原生视觉冲击

Require:

- a decisive native action, texture, form, structure, sound or finished effect
- visual attack as the main readable event
- any ASMR or transient tied to a visible source and exact contact
- a peak stronger than an ordinary demo or beauty shot

Reject a generic lifestyle scene, slow product push, routine handling or VFX that hides a stronger native event.

### Route 2: 口播语义冲击

Require:

- one semantic mechanism and one short line
- directed performance: pace, stress, distance and relationship to camera
- a visible action that the line triggers, predicts, counts, contradicts or completes
- product reveal on the semantic or vocal peak

Reject ordinary narration, generic praise, detachable viral lines and speech that can be removed without changing meaning.

### Route 3: 通用爆点骨架 × 商品专属峰值

Require:

- one named chassis and one dominant motion/contrast vector
- a product-specific peak role
- at least one current-product feature—shape, action, sound, material, name, phrase or scene—rewriting the chassis
- a stable product reveal rather than a late Packshot

Reject spectacle whose full action chain survives unchanged after replacing the product with any unrelated object.

## Ranking Dimensions

After the gates pass, compare candidates on:

- **Stop power**: immediacy and mobile readability of the first strike
- **Peak delta**: change in speed, scale, direction, sound, meaning, state or viewpoint
- **Product fit**: dependence on actual product resources
- **Product peak binding**: whether removing the product breaks the Hook
- **Causal/semantic link**: whether setup naturally leads to reveal
- **Audiovisual precision**: whether motion, sound, speech and reveal land together
- **Believability**: intentional reality level and only one anomaly when reality-amplified
- **Feasibility**: one clean five-second chain with stable product identity
- **Continuation fit**: usable hold, cover, whip, light, blackout or continued motion
- **Asset efficiency**: no unnecessary recurring people, props, rooms or voices
- **Claim risk**: no unsupported price, quantity, performance, comparison or endorsement

## One Winner Per Route

Generate 2–3 candidates inside each route. Keep the highest-scoring passing candidate from each route.

The three winners must not repeat the same action chain with only a changed room, actor, line, color or camera angle. When two winners use the same attention mechanism, require different dominant grammar and peak role.

Do not let the route contract force a weak result. Rewrite within that route using a different product resource, attention mechanism or execution grammar until it passes the same Heavy Hook gate.

## Dynamic Recommendation

Recommend the strongest of the three winners, not a fixed route and not automatically the most spectacular one.

Use this order:

1. stronger stop power and peak delta
2. tighter product peak binding
3. clearer causal or semantic setup
4. lower explanation cost
5. better audiovisual synchronization
6. higher generation stability
7. fewer continuity assets and lower claim risk

Use product priors only as tie-breakers:

- native action, texture, sound or finished effect → route 1
- generic appearance but strong routine, pain, identity or phrase → route 2
- explicit VFX/spectacle request or readable light, signal, energy, scale or metaphor → route 3

## Failure Patterns

- First 0.6 seconds contain preparation, establishing footage or a gentle camera move.
- Product is visible immediately but nothing later exceeds the opening.
- Product floats in soft light, rotates, or appears through particles with no rupture.
- Three options use the same mechanism with cosmetic changes.
- All options default to giant products, portals, explosions or particles.
- Product appears only as a blur, occluded object, unstable package or Packshot.
- Generic spectacle has no causal, acoustic or semantic path to the product.
- The chain adds setup, anomaly, demo, reaction, proof and transition as separate rewards.
- “ASMR” has no source, attack or rhythm; voice has no performance or visible job.
- Pain is resolved, challenge is proven, or the Hook becomes a mini sales body.
- The ending has no clean cut point for the following product segment.
