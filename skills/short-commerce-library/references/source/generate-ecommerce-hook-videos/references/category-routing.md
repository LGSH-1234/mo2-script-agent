## Route By Product Resource

Use category only as a prior. Inspect the actual product and assign the useful signals.

| Signal | Look for | Useful directions |
|---|---|---|
| `soundable` | tear, snap, click, pour, pop, impact, friction, magnet, mechanism | decisive action, close ASMR, rhythm, silence contrast |
| `texture-rich` | fiber, foam, liquid, food, metal, glass, embossing, reflection | macro, touch, practical physics, light |
| `transformable` | open, fold, expand, peel, assemble, change state | peak reveal, prediction reversal, match cut |
| `mechanical` | button, hinge, rotation, latch, moving part | tabletop precision, impact, visible mechanism |
| `effect-first` | finished atmosphere or state stops attention | result-first, environment reveal, light transition |
| `vfx-readable` | signal, energy, data, sound, optics, projection, internal structure | tracked graphics, spatial response, one VFX system |
| `repeatable` | packs, units, modules, colors | count rhythm, jump cut, array, one-to-many |
| `wearable` | meaning changes on a body | identity, direct gaze, POV, movement |
| `pourable` | flow, spray, mix, fizz, foam | macro, time manipulation, sound |
| `pain-linked` | instantly recognizable friction | near-miss, repeated failure, time pressure, spoken interruption |
| `reactionable` | safe person or pet reaction is natural | reaction first, delayed reveal, harmless humor |
| `identity-led` | taste, aspiration, group, self-image | character, atmosphere, direct address |
| `context-led` | object is generic until placed in a routine | scene, POV, pain, voice, format hijack |
| `phrase-ready` | supplied name, event or phrase has a physical reading | literalization, extreme entrance, scale, transaction event |

Classify native Hook strength:

- **Strong**: silhouette, finished effect, motion, sound or transformation already stops attention. Prefer route 1.
- **Medium**: useful tactile, acoustic, mechanical or contextual behavior needs performance or amplification. Compare routes 1 and 2.
- **Weak**: static or generic without context. Prefer route 2 or a tightly bound route 3; do not invent a selling point.

## Recommend One Of Three Routes

Recommend dynamically after all three route winners exist.

### Recommend Route 1: 商品原生视觉冲击

Favor when the product has a strong native action, texture, structure, sound, silhouette or finished visual result. Also favor when exact product fidelity matters and a generic spectacle would hide it.

### Recommend Route 2: 口播语义冲击

Favor when visual resources are generic but a routine, pain point, identity, name, supplied phrase, count or reaction becomes legible in one sentence. Require a visible action that answers the line.

### Recommend Route 3: 通用爆点骨架 × 商品专属峰值

Favor when the user explicitly asks for VFX, spectacle, extreme entrance, giant scale or metaphor; when the product has readable signal/energy/light/space semantics; or when native resources are too weak to create a heavy opening alone.

Do not recommend route 3 merely because it looks expensive. Do not recommend route 1 merely because it is safer to generate.

## Category Priors

### Paper Products And Tissues

- Resources: pull, sharp tear, unfold, flutter, layer, embossing, package slap, quantity rhythm, imminent spill.
- Route 1: decisive pull/tear, macro fibers, one-to-many reveal.
- Route 2: count-up, interruption, occupied-hands or ordinary-friction line.
- Route 3: paper storm, environment takeover, scale inversion or lens wipe, with the product causing or resolving the peak.

### Towels, Bedding And Textiles

- Resources: pinch, press, rub, twist, wring, snap open, drape, compression, fibers, water/fabric interaction.
- Route 1: macro texture, fabric whip, close snap, droplets, hand choreography.
- Route 2: short command, disbelief or routine recognition synchronized to one fabric action.
- Route 3: fabric becomes a wave, curtain, terrain or frame-cover transition; preserve recognizable weave and form at peak.

### Lighting And Decorative Home Products

- Resources: switch, shadow, projection, glow, silhouette, reflection, room transformation.
- Route 1: result-first finished room, switch-on, forced perspective, practical light transition.
- Route 2: question or contradiction such as naming the apparent moon only at reveal.
- Route 3: room-scale light takeover, indoor/outdoor alignment or scale inversion. When the product is already the spectacle, avoid a second unrelated transformation.

### Consumer Electronics And Accessories

- Resources: click, magnet, plug, rotate, press, screen, lens, sound, signal, data, energy, internal structure.
- Route 1: precision tabletop, component snap, product macro, silence contrast.
- Route 2: clipped instruction, count, interruption or knowledge-asymmetry line.
- Route 3: `real operation → invisible mechanism becomes visible → environment responds once`.

### Food And Snacks

- Resources: package tear, crunch, break, pour, steam, texture, crumbs, first reaction.
- Prefer decisive opening, high-speed pivotal motion, close sound, incomplete consumption and reaction-first setups.
- Do not turn the Hook into a taste, quantity or nutrition proof.

### Beverages And Mixable Products

- Resources: cap, ice, bubbles, pour, swirl, fizz, splash, temperature atmosphere.
- Prefer macro, sound-first, color transition, ordinary thirst/routine and one controlled liquid spectacle.

### Beauty, Skincare And Personal Care

- Resources: texture, reflection, cap/pump, hand gesture, mirror, routine, identity, private friction.
- Prefer macro, direct-to-camera interruption, mirror reveal, restrained reaction or semantic tension.
- Keep body, efficacy and treatment results out of the Hook.

### Apparel, Shoes, Bags And Wearable Accessories

- Resources: silhouette, zipper, clasp, fabric, walking rhythm, outfit, identity.
- Let person, movement and direct gaze carry the Hook when wearing changes meaning. Use action match, POV or one identity transition.

### Home Organization, Cleaning And Household Goods

- Resources: clutter, search, repetition, obstruction, capacity mystery, stack, unfold, escalating mess, near-miss.
- Establish the friction, pattern or impending contact; leave organization, absorption and cleaning proof to the following segment.

### Kitchen Appliances And Tools

- Resources: button, lid, latch, rotation, assembly, impact, steam, mechanism anticipation.
- End at activation or first contact. Prefer tabletop precision, sound-first, chain reaction or a safe extreme entrance.

### Maternal, Baby And Pet Products

- Resources: gentle routine, caregiver recognition, quiet cue, gaze, curiosity, safe reaction.
- Keep the Hook harmless. Never use child danger, animal fear, restraint or fabricated safety/development claims.

### Jewelry, Eyewear And Fragrance

- Resources: material macro, light sweep, reflection, lens view, hand/body movement, identity, atmosphere.
- For fragrance, use association and reaction rather than presenting a metaphor as proof of scent or effect.

### Sports And Outdoor Products

- Resources: motion tension, POV, environment contrast, challenge setup, impact sound.
- Stop before proof. Keep realistic stunts professional and controlled; otherwise make spectacle clearly fantastical.

### Health And Wellness Products

- Resources: daily routine friction, empathy, identity, habit, restrained speech.
- Avoid diagnosis, treatment, fear, guaranteed outcome, body shame or implied professional endorsement.

## VFX Affinity

Raise VFX priority when an invisible concept can be visualized, the supplied phrase has a physical metaphor, the silhouette survives the effect, or the user explicitly requests spectacle.

Lower VFX priority when product-native action/result is already stronger, text or packaging is likely to drift, the effect implies performance, or the product would appear only as a disconnected Packshot.

## Sparse-Input Fallback

1. Inspect supplied imagery for silhouette, material, packaging, action, reflection, texture and likely sound.
2. Assign native strength and useful signals.
3. Build route 1 from one native action or result.
4. Build route 2 from one routine, pain, identity, count or grounded phrase.
5. Build route 3 from one universal chassis whose peak must be rewritten for the product.
6. Never invent promotion, quantity, performance or category claims to rescue a weak route.
