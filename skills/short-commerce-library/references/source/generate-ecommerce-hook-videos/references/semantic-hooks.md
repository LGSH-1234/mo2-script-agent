## Route Contract

Build a language-led candidate as:

```text
one semantic mechanism × one short line × one visible response × one product peak role
```

Let wording create conflict, information gap, identity, rhythm or momentum that the five-second image completes. Do not copy a viral sentence and attach an unrelated product. Reject ordinary product description even when it is enthusiastic.

The line must lose important meaning if removed from the footage, and the footage must visibly answer or embody it.

Language may arrive through spoken direct address, off-camera speech, one call-and-response, a short post-produced phrase, a number, or a sentence completed by the visual reveal.

## Semantic Mechanisms

### Information Gap

Open one unanswered question and let the product, action or following cut answer it.

Forms: `先别看答案。` `你猜这里面是什么？` `我最后悔的是没早点换。`

### Belief Conflict

Challenge one familiar assumption and pair it with an immediate corrective action.

Forms: `你一直用反了。` `不是越慢越有质感。` `先别急着按。`

Avoid unsupported category accusations or factual superiority.

### Replacement Frame

Establish a familiar object, routine or choice, remove it, then place the product into the same visual role.

Forms: `我把这个，换成了这个。` `以前是这样。现在——`

Keep comparisons structural or behavioral; do not invent a competitor claim.

### Personal Experience

Use a short experience statement to create expectation or realization.

Forms: `我用了30天，最没想到的是这个。` `我差点放弃它。`

The duration may function as a Hook frame. Do not add measurable results, guarantees, medical effects or a fabricated testimonial identity.

### Identity And Empathy

Name a recognizable behavior, routine or frustration.

Forms: `手上总是腾不出来的人，看这里。` `每次出门都忘一个东西？`

Use visible behavior rather than demographics, shame or fear.

### Knowledge Asymmetry

Direct attention to one overlooked action, detail or sound.

Forms: `真正该听的是这一声。` `别看正面，看这里。`

Avoid `同类都含有`、`行业都在隐瞒` and other category-wide claims without a supplied basis.

### Count And Rhythm

Use numbers as audible choreography, not unsupported quantity claims.

Forms: `一、二、三——开。` `三秒，听完这一声。`

Synchronize every number with an appearance, impact or state change.

### Urgency And Momentum

Use time pressure or trend language to interrupt attention.

Forms: `它正在爆火，但先看这一秒。` `再晚一秒就接不住了。`

General `正在爆火` may work as a Hook frame. Do not turn it into ranking, sales, scarcity, countdown or price without user grounding.

### Secret Tone

Use close, restrained delivery and one pause.

Forms: `这句本来不该说。` `靠近点，听。`

Do not imply illegal access, private disclosure or institutional confidentiality.

### Reaction And Realization

Let one line name a visible surprise.

Forms: `等等，它怎么自己亮了？` `原来声音在这里。`

Specify gaze, gesture, object and reveal; reject generic praise such as `太好用了`.

### Supplied Phrase Literalization

Turn a grounded phrase into one physical event and speak the link on the peak.

Examples: `直降` becomes vertical descent; `到手` becomes a catch; `上新` becomes an object dropping into frame. Exact promotion, price, quantity and scarcity remain fact dependencies.

## Visual Grounding

Make every important line do at least one visible job:

- trigger an action
- point to a detail
- establish a comparison frame
- predict an event
- synchronize a count, tear, switch, drop or impact
- create a question that the product answers
- name the product on the stressed beat

For peak reveal, begin with a person, familiar object, concealed shape, sound, problem or incomplete sentence; reveal the product on the stressed word, impact, switch, removal, catch or camera turn.

For result-first, let the line question or name the transformed atmosphere and let the product become the visible cause.

Do not rely on downstream content to make an otherwise incoherent five-second line meaningful.

## Performance And Text Mode

Read [Audio And Voice](audio-voice.md) and define delivery, pace, stressed word, distance and synchronization.

Prefer speech when timing, secrecy, disbelief, command or interruption matters. Prefer post-produced text when spelling, price or a number must remain exact.

Use one spoken sentence or one short call-and-response. Keep on-screen text to one short phrase or number. Do not combine full speech, subtitles, ASMR, music and multiple visual anomalies.

## Selection Rules

Prefer this route when:

- the product is visually generic but gains meaning from context or routine
- a supplied name, phrase, identity or promotion has a strong physical reading
- the reveal needs a question, command, count, contradiction or replacement frame
- vocal performance can become the timing spine

Lower priority when product-native motion or sound is already stronger, the line needs long explanation, or the visible action does not answer it.

Apply exact commercial, health, authority, comparison and identity limits from [Safety Boundaries](safety-boundaries.md).
